**Findings and dispositions**

**M3T0-F1 — MAJOR — VERIFIED: the missed-crossing estimate is not a bound.**

References: `D/t0_decay.py:224–231`; `P/DR-0029-m3-t0-proposals.md:92,110`; `docs/science/validation.md:784`.

Reproduce with `.venv/bin/python S/counterexamples.py`. Shift the sample phase so perigee lies at the midpoint of a ten-second step, preserving the 250 × 800 km, 51.6° configuration. Independent Kepler propagation gives a geodetic dip of **4.98764 m**, exceeding **4.16525 m**. Using the spike's actual J2+drag force on the same midpoint construction still gives **4.81710 m**. A circular inclined orbit gives **0.45162 m** although the proposed expression is zero.

For spherical Kepler radius, perigee curvature is `μe/r_p² = ae n²/(1−e)²`, already larger than `ae n²`. Geodetic height adds latitude-dependent curvature. A fixed-phase, 0.5-second sampled maximum of 1.564 m cannot establish an upper bound over sample phases.

Recommended correction: retain the measured maximum as a measurement, replace the bound with a justified bound on geodetic-height curvature over the admitted domain, and test shifted phases/eccentricities. Update the dependent proposal and validation claims. **Disposition: open; no fix made.**

**M3T0-F2 — MAJOR — VERIFIED: P1 does not guarantee the stage-domain/termination behavior claimed for every admissible run.**
