**Mathematical assessment.** Let `t=2^-1074`, `eta0=t/2`, `u=2^-53` and `D=cd*area` in exact arithmetic.

For C1 with finite positive rounded `P`, `P>=t`. If `b>0`, `b>=t`, so `b-eta0>=eta0>0`. Bounding `D/P` by the minimum of its positive-mass/product constraints, then multiplying by `max((1+u)/b,1/(b-eta0))`, safely covers both normal and subnormal quotient rounding. For C2, positive mass requires positive intermediate `Q`; both are at least t. The stated clamped lower bounds for Q and mass follow by taking the smaller of relative and absolute rounding lower bounds and the independent t lower bound. All denominators remain positive. Their combination with `phi/b` is conservative and non-increasing in positive binary64 b.

At `b=0`, C1 gives `mass/P<=eta0`, with the tie rounding to even zero. It is reachable precisely when `P>=2` for some positive mass; with `P=33`, masses k*t for k=1…16 give zero and k=17 gives t. C2 at zero gives zero mass and supplies no positive vehicle. Since every positive binary64 mass is at least t, `D/mass<=D/t` exactly. Thus `R(0)=max(D/t,R(t))` is conservative, and joins the positive-b bound monotonically. The extra max is needed for the advertised monotonic extension, not for the direct mass inequality. This is a proof from the rounding premises, not an inference from a sweep.

`B_R=phi/R` is positive; its downward-rounded Decimal representation avoids binary64 underflow. Finite binary64 inputs keep its exponent within the Decimal context's range. For positive b with `R=phi/b`, the function returns that same float b, without recomputing it. Both envelope parts receive B_R. The force coefficient's absolute allowance contains `D/mass`, `area/mass` and `1/mass`; the latter two are bounded by R/cd and R/D, with the explicit supplied shape parameters. “Only through the ratio” should be understood as this substitution, not as shape-independence of all underflow behavior.

Independently expanding the coefficient chain gives `|k| <= (1+u)^3*(D/mass)*abs(rho_hat)/2 + W`, where `W=eta0*[1+(1+u)^3*D/mass+(1+u)^2*area/mass+(1+u)/mass]`. Multiplication by 0.5 is exact unless it underflows; each later multiplication/division transports the preceding absolute errors through the remaining factors. This accounts for division by subnormal mass rather than applying a relative-only error there. The rounded relative velocity is bounded by `(1+u)^2*S+sqrt(2)*(1+u)*eta0`; the dot/sqrt allowance bounds its norm product, and the last two products retain their absolute errors. These produce the separate relative, W, linear-speed and constant terms, without a missing zero-b division. The zero-b correction changes the missing ratio inequality, not these operation counts.
