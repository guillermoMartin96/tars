# I104-02: proposed owner disposition (prepared under I-105; NOT decided)

**Status: I104-02 is OPEN, and OB-16 and OB-17 are not MET.** This document proposes text for the
owner. Nothing in it is granted, and nothing in the evidence was changed to prepare it.
The inventory is `historical-gap-inventory.json` in this directory. It is generated by
`../scripts/gap_inventory.py` from each execution's own sealed files.

## 1. Why an owner decision is unavoidable

v11 asks for records that some executions never made:
- **OB-16:** "ENV (DL-5) records for every gate-evidence run from commit A".
- **OB-17:** "the §3.16 run-record provenance fields complete for every gate-evidence run (else
  stop 18)".
- **§3.11 ENV:** "a missing record for a gate-evidence run is a stop".

Those executions already happened (§2). A record cannot be made afterwards for a run that is over,
and records from other runs do not establish its environment. As worded, therefore, OB-16 and OB-17
cannot become MET for the S2 history, however complete every later run is. There are two ways
forward:
- a bounded owner exception or amendment (§4), recommended;
- restarting S2 evidence from a new commit A′ on a new history (§5).

> 1. The executions RED-ORIGINAL, RED-SUPPLEMENTAL, I101-CAMPAIGN, I102-CAMPAIGN and
>    I103-CAMPAIGN [and D-SCRIPTS-AT-BC], as identified with their paths and hashes in

>    no environment is inferred for them.
> 2. For OB-16, OB-17, §3.11 ENV ("a missing record for a gate-evidence run is a stop") and §3.15

>    item 1, and no other execution.

>    `~/tars-evidence/I-105/REPLACEMENT-EXECUTIONS.md`:

>    - the I-105 green run;

The bracketed parts depend on how the owner classifies D-SCRIPTS-AT-BC:
- **If it is gate evidence,** keep the brackets' content. The I-105 derivation run is its

- **If not,** delete the bracketed parts.

**What changes under §4.** OB-16 and OB-17 could then be assessed MET by a new §5.2 review of the
corrected candidate, from the replacement executions together with every complete record. I104-02
would be closed by that review, not by the author.

## 5. Alternatives

- **(B) A wording amendment to OB-16 and OB-17, with the same effect.** Append to both rows: "except
  the executions retained as disclosed nonconformances in owner determination [ref], under its
  conditions." This uses the same list and conditions as §4.
- **(C) No exception.** OB-16 and OB-17 stay FAILED, and S2 cannot pass §5.2 on this history. Meeting
  the requirement literally would need new S2 evidence history from a new commit A′, which means a
  new branch and a re-run of A, BC and the rest. I-105 does not authorize that.
